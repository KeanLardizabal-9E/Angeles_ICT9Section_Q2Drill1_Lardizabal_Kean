Kean's Guide to Becoming a Badminton Star

What is badminton?

  • A fast-paced racket sport where two opposing players (singles) or two pairs (doubles) hit a feathered or synthetic projectile called a shuttlecock back and forth over a high net.
  • Score points by hitting the shuttlecock (birdie) across the net so that it lands inside your opponent's court or forces your opponent to make an error and fail to return it properly.

The Basics

  • Hold the racket with a relaxed grip (forehand and backhand) and practice quick, light steps to stay balanced and reach the shuttlecock easily.
  • Start every rally with a legal underhand serve, keeping the shuttlecock below your waist level when hit.
  • Master fundamental strokes like the clear, the drop, and the smash.

Tips and Tricks

  • Always return to the center court area between shots, staying light on the balls of your feet with your racket held high.
  • Use the same wrist action and preparation setup for different strokes so your opponent cannot predict whether you are playing a drop shot, clear, or smash.
  • Direct your shots toward your opponent's non-dominant side (usually their backhand) or right at their body to force an awkward return.
